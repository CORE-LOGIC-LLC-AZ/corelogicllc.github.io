ReelDispatch

Built for creators who already export locally

Skip the SaaS upload hop. ReelDispatch talks to platform APIs from your machine and keeps credentials in the OS keychain.

One compose → three platforms

Pick an MP4, set captions and per-account overrides, and enqueue uploads to connected YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram accounts.

Schedule & tray queue

Schedule posts for later. Minimize to tray so the worker keeps running. Pause and resume from the tray menu.

First comments

After a successful YouTube or Instagram publish, optionally post your first comment automatically.

Local API & MCP

Optional localhost HTTP API and MCP adapter so generation scripts can queue videos without opening the UI.

Watched folder

Drop finished exports into a folder; stable files are ingested with your default caption and accounts.

Licensed desktop product

Lemon Squeezy licensing for monthly or lifetime Pro. Tokens and license keys stay on your device.

How it works

01

Connect accounts

OAuth into YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. Secrets for TikTok/Meta stay in our broker — never in the binary.

02

Compose & queue

Import one or many MP4s, validate with FFmpeg, set captions, schedule if needed, and enqueue.

03

Dispatch

The background worker uploads while you keep working — or while the app sits in the tray.

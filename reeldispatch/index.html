How it works

01

Connect accounts

OAuth into YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. Secrets for TikTok/Meta stay in our broker — never in the binary. Free allows one account per platform.

02

Compose & publish

Import MP4s, validate with FFmpeg, and Publish Now on Free. Pro adds JSON sidecars, scheduling, watched-folder ingest, and API/MCP enqueue.

03

Dispatch

The background worker uploads while you keep working — or, on Pro, while the app sits in the tray with scheduled posts.

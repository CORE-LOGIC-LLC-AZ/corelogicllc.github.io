Capy Merge Spa

Sink into a pocket onsen with chill capybaras. Merge friends, expand your spa, and soak up calm vibes — no fail states, just steam and smiles.

Free · iOS · Casual / Idle

How to play Privacy policy

Features

Merge spa friends

Buy Level 1 capybaras with Yuzu Coins, then drag matching levels together to unlock five adorable spa looks — from Capy Lemon to Spa Royalty.

Idle onsen earnings

Capys earn coins on the grid and while you’re away (offline earnings capped for fair play). Grow the board from 4×4 toward a roomier 6×6 spa.

Optional boosts

Watch a short rewarded ad for Spa Booster (2× income for a few minutes) or Double Relax to double offline earnings — never required to progress.

Cozy polish

Lo-fi vibes, gentle haptics, Outfit Album, shareable spa cards, and accessibility options (font scale, high contrast, reduce motion, music toggle).

How to play

  1. Tap Buy to place a starter capy on the hot spring grid
  2. Drag two of the same level together to merge into the next tier
  3. Collect Yuzu Coins over time — reinvest to buy more friends or expand the spa
  4. Open the Album to track unlocked looks; long-press a capy to share

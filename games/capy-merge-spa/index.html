In the spa

Features

Merge spa friends

Buy Level 1 capybaras with Yuzu Coins, then drag matching levels together to unlock six spa looks — from Capy Lemon to mint Onsen Kami — plus sakura, yukimi, maple, and golden guests. Gumdrop faces, rounded snouts, blinks, and the occasional breath.

Idle onsen earnings

Capys earn coins on the grid and while you’re away (offline earnings capped for fair play). Grow the board from 4×4 toward a roomier 6×6 spa.

Optional boosts

Watch a short rewarded ad for Spa Booster (2× income for a few minutes), Double Relax to double offline earnings, or a Yuzu snack in the shop (capped at 3 per day) — never required to progress.

Cozy polish

Lo-fi vibes, gentle haptics, Album silhouettes until you unlock a look, seasonal outfits for Yuzu (Cherry Blossom, Snowy Onsen, Autumn Leaf), native share-sheet spa cards, and accessibility options (font scale, high contrast, reduce motion, music toggle).

How to play

  1. Tap Buy to place a starter capy on the hot spring grid
  2. Drag two of the same level together to merge into the next tier
  3. Collect Yuzu Coins over time — reinvest to buy more friends or expand the spa
  4. Open the Album to track unlocked looks (silhouettes until you find them); long-press a capy to share via the system sheet

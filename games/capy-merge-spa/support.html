Quick FAQ

  • Progress missing? Saves stay on this device. Reinstalling or switching phones starts a fresh spa unless you restored from a device backup that included the app data.
  • Ads? Rewarded ads for Spa Booster, Double Relax, and a Yuzu snack (shop, capped at 3 per day) are optional. You can play without watching them.
  • Purchases? There are no real-money in-app purchases. Seasonal shop skins (Cherry Blossom, Snowy Onsen, Autumn Leaf) cost Yuzu Coins earned in play.
  • Sound or haptics? Open Settings in the app to toggle music, adjust accessibility options, and reduce motion.
  • Privacy / data request? See our privacy policy, or email admin@corelogic.cc with “Capy Merge Spa Privacy” in the subject.

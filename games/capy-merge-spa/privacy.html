Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 16, 2026 · Published by Core Logic LLC
App bundle ID: cc.corelogic.capymeregespa

This Privacy Policy describes how Core Logic LLC (“we,” “us”) handles information in connection with the mobile game Capy Merge Spa (the “App”).

Summary

  • We do not require an account
  • Game progress is stored on your device
  • The App shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising identifiers under Google’s policies
  • We do not sell your personal information

On-device game data

The App stores gameplay data locally on your device (for example: Yuzu Coins, spa grid, unlock progress, and settings such as music and accessibility preferences). This data stays on your device unless you back up or transfer the device using your platform’s tools.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Optional rewarded video ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers (including IDFA on iOS if you grant permission)
  • Advertising data and ad interaction data
  • Approximate technical information needed to serve and measure ads

On iOS, you may see an App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can allow or deny tracking. You can also reset advertising identifiers in system Settings.

Google’s privacy information:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Share feature

If you use Share, the App may create an image and open the system share sheet. We do not upload that image to our servers. What happens next depends on the app you choose (Messages, Photos, etc.).

Information we do not collect

We do not intentionally collect: name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, payment card details, or government ID for this App. Version 1.0 does not offer real-money in-app purchases through Apple.

How we use information

  • Operate and save your spa progress on device
  • Provide optional rewarded ads that help keep the App free
  • Improve stability and features over time (using aggregate / non-identifying feedback you send to support)

Children

The App is designed as a general-audience cozy game suitable for a broad age range. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own profiles. Advertising is provided by Google; parents and guardians can use device-level controls (Screen Time, App Tracking Transparency, etc.).

Data retention

Local game saves remain until you delete the App or clear its data. Advertising data retention is governed by Google.

Your choices

  • Decline ATT tracking when prompted
  • Skip rewarded ads (boosts and doubling are optional)
  • Delete the App to remove local saves
  • Contact us to ask questions about this policy

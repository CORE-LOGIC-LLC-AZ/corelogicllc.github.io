Offline Local Card Games

PeerDeck

At the table

Built for real-world gatherings

True offline autonomy

Fully operational in dead-zones, airplanes, subways, or remote trips — no cellular data, Wi‑Fi router, or online account required.

Hardware-to-hardware

Nearby devices discover each other automatically over Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework using local Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth.

Premium card-table UX

A dark, tactile interface built natively with SwiftUI — low-latency sync, haptic feedback, and smooth in-person play.

How it works

01

Host a table

Tap Host Table on one device to start a session and share a room code.

02

Join locally

Other players tap Join Table, pick a nearby host, and enter the code.

03

Play in sync

The host selects a game module and every device stays synchronized for the round.

Launch games

Trick-taking · 4, 6, or 8 players

28 (Thuruppu)

Classic South Asian card game. Players sit in partnerships, bid on tricks, and set a hidden trump suit. The round dealer rotates each hand; bidding opens at the seat after the dealer.

  • 4, 6, or 8 players at the same table
  • Host-authoritative state sync for fair, in-person play

Color match · 2–10 players

One

Fast color-matching party game for the whole table. Match color or value, use action cards to skip and reverse, and shout ONE! when you are down to a single card.

  • 2 to 10 players at the same table
  • Peek, challenge, and draw piles stay in sync on every phone

More game modules are planned — one app, many tables.

Free to play

  • PeerDeck is free — no in-app purchases in this version
  • Light banner ads may appear on the dashboard, lobby, and game selection screens when your device is online
  • Occasional full-screen ads may appear between rounds (not during play)
  • When you are offline, ads do not load — hosting, joining, and gameplay continue normally

Get started

  1. Tap Host Table on one device to start a session
  2. Other players tap Join Table to find nearby hosts
  3. Enter the room code, pick a game, and play together in sync

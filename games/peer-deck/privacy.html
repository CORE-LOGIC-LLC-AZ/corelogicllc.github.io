PeerDeck

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026 · Published by Core Logic LLC
App bundle ID: cc.corelogic.peerdeck

PeerDeck is a local multiplayer card game for Apple devices. Gameplay works offline over local wireless and Bluetooth. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Summary

  • Does not require an account
  • Does not sell user data
  • Uses local network, Bluetooth, and Bonjour only to discover and connect nearby players during a session
  • Uses the device name only as the local display name shown to nearby players (e.g. "Alex's iPhone")
  • Does not transmit gameplay data to external servers
  • Does not include in-app purchases in this version
  • May show Google AdMob ads when the device is online (see below)

Local multiplayer connectivity

PeerDeck uses Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework to let nearby devices find each other and play together on the same table. This requires permission to use your device's local network and Bluetooth radios while a session is active.

Discovery and gameplay sync happen directly between players' devices. Session data is not uploaded to Core Logic LLC servers or third-party services during normal play.

Device display name

When you host or join a table, your device's system name may appear to other nearby players (for example, "Sarah's iPhone 15 Table"). This name is used only for local identification during the session and is not collected or stored by us on external servers.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When your device has an internet connection, PeerDeck may show light ads via Google AdMob:

  • Banner ads on the dashboard, lobby, and game selection screens
  • Occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads between rounds — not during card play
  • Ads do not load while offline; hosting, joining, and gameplay continue normally

Ads are requested as non-personalized in this version. PeerDeck does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt for advertising.

When ads load, Google AdMob may process device identifiers and advertising-related data under Google’s policies. For details, see:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Data we do not collect

PeerDeck does not collect:

  • Account credentials or user IDs
  • Location data
  • Contacts or browsing history
  • Usage analytics or crash diagnostics sent to Core Logic LLC
  • Payment or billing information

Any advertising-related data processed when ads load is handled by Google AdMob as described above, not by Core Logic LLC gameplay servers (PeerDeck has none for play sync).

Children

PeerDeck is intended for general audiences (App Store age rating 4+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is not designed as a Made for Kids experience.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features change (for example, if analytics, accounts, tracking, or in-app purchases are added). The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when that happens.

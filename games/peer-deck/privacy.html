PeerDeck

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026 · Published by Core Logic LLC

PeerDeck is an offline, local multiplayer card game for Apple devices. This policy describes how the app handles information at v1.0 launch.

Summary

  • Does not require an account
  • Does not sell user data
  • Does not use third-party analytics or ads at v1.0 launch (MonetizationConfig.adsEnabled = false)
  • Uses local network, Bluetooth, and Bonjour only to discover and connect nearby players during a session
  • Uses the device name only as the local display name shown to nearby players (e.g. "Alex's iPhone")
  • Does not transmit gameplay data to external servers
  • Does not store payment information (no in-app purchases at launch)

Local multiplayer connectivity

PeerDeck uses Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework to let nearby devices find each other and play together on the same table. This requires permission to use your device's local network and Bluetooth radios while a session is active.

Discovery and gameplay sync happen directly between players' devices. Session data is not uploaded to Core Logic LLC servers or third-party services during normal play.

Device display name

When you host or join a table, your device's system name may appear to other nearby players (for example, "Sarah's iPhone 15 Table"). This name is used only for local identification during the session and is not collected or stored by us on external servers.

Data we do not collect

At v1.0 launch, PeerDeck does not collect:

  • Account credentials or user IDs
  • Location data
  • Contacts or browsing history
  • Usage analytics or diagnostics sent to third parties
  • Payment or billing information

Children

PeerDeck is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features change (for example, if analytics, accounts, in-app purchases, or advertising are added in a future release). The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when that happens.
